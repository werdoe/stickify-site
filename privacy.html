Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Stickify is made by Mohammad Raiyan Asaral. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Stickify.

What we collect

Nothing. Stickify has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send your photos, stickers or any other data to us or to anyone else.

Your photos

When you choose a photo or take one, Stickify reads it on your iPhone to find the subject and make the sticker. The photo crop, the cut-out and your stickers are stored only on your device, in Stickify's own storage, so the iPhone's sticker drawer can show them. Deleting a sticker or the app removes them.

Camera and Photos

Stickify asks for the camera only when you tap Camera, and asks to add to your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos. It never reads your library in the background; the photo picker shows Stickify only the photos you pick.

Purchases

Stickify Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether Stickify Pro is active.

Children

Stickify does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version collects any information (for example, an optional community area for sharing stickers), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
