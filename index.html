Common questions

Where do my stickers show up?

Open any chat, tap the emoji key on the keyboard, then tap the stickers button and choose Stickify. They work in Messages, WhatsApp, Messenger and other apps that support stickers. In Messages you can also tap + › Stickers.

Stickify isn't in my sticker drawer

Open Stickify once and save at least one sticker. Then, in the sticker drawer, swipe to the end of the app row, tap Edit (or the three dots) and make sure Stickify is turned on.

The cut-out missed a part, or kept too much

Open the sticker and tap Touch Up. Use Erase to remove stray bits and Restore to bring back a missed hand or hat. The Edges slider tightens or loosens the outline.

The face is too small in my sticker

In the editor, choose Upper Body or Close-Up under the preview. Stickify finds the face and zooms in so it stays clear at sticker size.

How do I restore Stickify Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Stickify › Settings › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Stickify, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Cut-outs and effects are made on your iPhone, and your photos and stickers never leave it. See the Privacy Policy.
